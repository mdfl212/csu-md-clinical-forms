Designed for learning

These are self-contained HTML applications. No account or backend is required. Form data is saved locally in your browser and remains on your device.

  • Use Chrome or Edge for speech recognition.
  • Open a form to begin; data autosaves locally.
  • Educational use for CSU SOM MD201–MD202.
